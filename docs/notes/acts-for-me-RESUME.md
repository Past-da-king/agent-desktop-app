## Design (as built so far)
- `shared/approval-policy.ts` (NEW, pure): `classifyCard(facts)` -> `{lane: 'him'|'agent', rule, why}`. Decided from asker (helper/project/routine are task askers; main, device, unknown are always him) and the REAL tool call stamped on the card (`apps_run` slug, `webapp_run` tool, `browser_fetch` method and host, `send_file`, shell commands, files, settings_set keys, app_action method, vault, upload...). Unknown tool or missing origin = him. For `request_approval` and `ask_user` (the tool asks in its own words) the text is only screened to send a card TO him (send, money, hands, secret words); it can never move one to the agent. Destructive commands aimed at root/home/system folders = him ("outside a task").
- `shared/card-state.ts` (NEW): meta keys on the card row: `callOrigin`, `asker`, `lane`, `laneRule`, `laneWhy`, `routedAt`, `notedAt`, `escalatedAt/Why`, `closedAt/Reason/Why`, `autoAnswer`. `needsHim(row)` = live (not decided, not closed) and not routed-pending: THE test for Needs you. `stampCard()`, `verdictOfRow()` (recomputed from the stamped callOrigin; the stored lane is only a second "him" vote).
- `shared/stale-card.ts` (NEW, pure): `staleReason(facts)` -> replaced | run-ended | answered-elsewhere | already-done. No waiter + 20 s grace first.
- `main/runtime/acts-for-me.ts` (NEW): class `ActsForMe` with `stamp`, `route` (batched note to main chat, 4 s), `waiting`, `answer` (hard safety: refuses him-lane, decided, closed, setting off, main's own cards; records `autoAnswer`; `escalate` allowed), `escalate`, `list`, `undo` (revert-setting; or withdraw a decline/answer: tells the asker and holds that asker's next cards for him 30 min), `sweep` (escalate routed cards after 10 min or when the setting is off; close stale cards; max 80 per pass), `close`.
- `main/tools/cards.ts` (NEW): main-agent-only tools `cards_waiting` and `card_answer` (also refuses helper/routine/project-chat contexts).
- `packages/core/src/model.ts`: `ApprovalOrigin {tool, input}`; `ToolContext.approve(summary, details?, scope?, origin?)` and `ask(question, options, card?, origin?)`.
- `packages/core/src/ipc.ts`: `Settings.actsForMe: boolean`, `DEFAULT_SETTINGS.actsForMe = true`.
- `main/runtime/toolset.ts`: `withOrigin(tools)` stamps the dispatched tool name + input on every approve/ask (applied in buildWorkerToolset and buildToolset); `ToolsetDeps.cards` adds the main agent's card tools. `main/vault/guard.ts`, `sdk-permissions.ts` (Claude Bash/Write/Read approve with `{tool: name, input}`), `cli-host.ts` forward the 4th argument.
